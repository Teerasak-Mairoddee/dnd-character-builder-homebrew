# DnD Character Builder (Homebrew)

A one-page character build sheet for a simplified D&D homebrew. Every roll is **d20 + one number** against the DC.

**Use it:** https://teerasak-mairoddee.github.io/dnd-character-builder-homebrew/

## What it does

- Spend ability points and pick trained skills, capped by level tier so characters stay balanced against the difficulty tiers.
- Roll a random name, or roll a random level (locked until you make a new character).
- Get inspiration from 12 class builds, or Gamble for a random legal build.
- Download a printable one-page PDF character sheet.
- Save and share builds with a copyable build code. Your last build is remembered in your browser.

## Tiers

| Levels | Ability points | Highest ability | Skill trainings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | 6 | +3 | 4 |
| 5–10 | 8 | +4 | 5 |
| 11–16 | 10 | +5 | 6 |
| 17–20 | 12 | +6 | 7 |

The whole app is `index.html`, with no build step.
